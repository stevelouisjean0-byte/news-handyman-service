A-05.2 Texting works

A photo saves a visit

Several customers started with a text and had a date set for the next day.

Call or text now

If you can, send a picture of the thing that needs doing — a leak under a sink, the gap in a piece of trim, the window that will not close. It often means arriving with the right material already on the truck.

For water actively running where it should not be, call rather than text, and shut the supply first.

  • What it is. A sentence and a photo beats a paragraph.
  • How urgent. Leaking now, or annoying for a month — both are fine, they just get scheduled differently.
  • Where in Apopka. So the route and the day can be planned around it.
  • Anything already tried. It saves repeating work that did not fix it.
