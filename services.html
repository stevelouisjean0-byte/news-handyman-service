Schedule of work

Nine lines of work, one set of hands.

Every item below is something customers have actually written about — this is the real book of work, not a list of everything a handyman could theoretically do.

01

Custom carpentry & cabinetry

Built for the room you have rather than the room a catalogue assumes. When a bathroom was too small for a double vanity, we made a custom one that took two sinks — and came the same day to fit it. Cabinets, vanities, built-ins, shelving and bespoke storage.

02

Trim & finish carpentry

Casing, baseboard, crown, chair rail and scribed returns. Intricate trim work is where the difference between adequate and right becomes visible, so it gets the time it needs — coped inside corners, tight mitres, nothing filled that should have been cut properly.

03

Water leak repair

One of the subjects reviewers name most. Found, traced and repaired — and because the same hands do the carpentry, the wall, the trim and the cabinet go back the way they were instead of waiting on a second trade.

04

Water heater repair

Another of the named specialties. No hot water is not a next-week problem, which is part of why the phone is answered around the clock.

05

Bathroom & kitchen fixtures

Sinks, faucets, vanities and the unglamorous connections behind them. Fitted level, sealed properly, and left clean.

06

Windows & doors

Repairs, adjustment and replacement. "He came right over gave a fair price for a window repair" is a customer's summary, and it is a fair description of how these go.

07

Grab bars & accessibility

Safety grab bars fixed into real structure, not just into drywall — the whole point of a grab bar being that it holds when someone's weight is on it. Handrails, thresholds and other adjustments that let a house keep working for the people in it.

08

Punch lists & contractor finish-out

The details a renovation left unfinished. More than one customer has called about exactly this: a newly renovated room with several small things still outstanding, and nobody coming back to do them.

09

Mower & small engine repair

Not what most people expect on a carpenter's list, but it is one of the things customers name most — so it belongs here rather than being tidied away.

Something not on this list? Call and ask — the answer is usually yes, and it is always honest.
